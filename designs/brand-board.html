Embroidery notes

Do

  • Keep to the five thread colors
  • Min. size 1.5″ for the icon, 2″ for the emblem
  • Use Ivory as the backing/knockout color on dark caps
  • Tonal (single-thread) versions for subtle colorways

Don't

  • Add gradients or fine detail that won't stitch
  • Recolor the marks outside the palette
  • Stretch or condense the wordmark
  • Outline the monogram — let the two colors read
