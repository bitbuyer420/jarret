05

Embroidery notes

Do

  • Keep to the five thread colors
  • Stitch animals tonal on colored crowns
  • Give the longhorn horns full width
  • Min. size 1.8″ so the legs and antlers read

Don't

  • Add gradients or fine fur detail that won't stitch
  • Recolor outside the palette
  • Crowd the antlers or horns against the seams
  • Mix an animal and a heritage mark on one cap
